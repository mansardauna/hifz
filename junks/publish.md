<h2>Ankabit LMS - Multi-Tenant Quran Academy Platform</h2>
<p><strong>Give every academy a professional digital learning workspace.</strong> Ankabit LMS combines academy websites, student admissions, Quran learning, subscriptions, live classrooms and tenant administration in one responsive Next.js application.</p>

<h2>Core Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Multi-tenant academy subdomains with tenant-specific branding and pages</li>
  <li>Visual GrapesJS page builder with reusable Islamic templates</li>
  <li>Uthmani Quran reader with Tajweed presentation, verse audio looping and recording</li>
  <li>Student admissions CRM with configurable forms and lead statuses</li>
  <li>Stripe and Moyasar tuition subscription checkout and webhook handling</li>
  <li>LiveKit audio/video classrooms for lessons and halaqah sessions</li>
  <li>Academy, tenant and platform administration workflows</li>
  <li>English and Arabic interfaces with RTL layout support</li>
  <li>PWA manifest and service-worker support for supported devices</li>
  <li>Email, WhatsApp, file-upload and analytics service integrations</li>
</ul>

<h2>Technology Stack</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Next.js 14 App Router, React 18 and TypeScript</li>
  <li>Prisma ORM with MySQL, PostgreSQL and MariaDB-compatible deployment options</li>
  <li>Tailwind CSS, Material UI, Emotion, Recharts and Lucide</li>
  <li>Docker deployment assets included; connect the app to your preferred reverse proxy</li>
</ul>

<h2>Requirements</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Node.js 20 or later and npm</li>
  <li>MySQL 8 or MariaDB 10.11 and a Redis instance for production deployment</li>
  <li>Valid credentials for the payment, LiveKit, storage and notification services you enable</li>
  <li>HTTPS for production payment, microphone, camera and subdomain workflows</li>
</ul>
